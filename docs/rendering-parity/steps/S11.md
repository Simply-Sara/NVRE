# S11–S16 — The unimplemented stages

These are the stages the pipeline does not have. They are written down with a
first step each, so that "1:1 for five games" has a known remaining cost
rather than a discovery waiting to happen. Every cell is **not started**.

## S11 — Animation

**What it owns:** `NiTimeController` and its subclasses, the keyframe data, and
per-frame bone transforms.

**State:** `crates/nvre-nif/src/anim.rs` is a one-line doc comment. The schema
defines the blocks (`base/anim.json`, 25 KB of them); nothing reads them.

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S11-1 | Every controller block in the corpus re-encodes, and the controller types present are enumerated per game | extend `nifprobe`'s type histogram |
| S11-2 | The text keys and the sequence list are read for a known animated mesh, and the frame count matches | `NiTextKeyExtraData` probe |
| S11-3 | One animation plays: a single node, a single translation track, sampled at a known time | unit test against a hand-computed interpolated value |

## S12 — Skinning

**What it owns:** `NiSkinInstance`, the bone list, the bone transforms, and the
skin-to-root transform.

**State:** in the schema (`base/core.json:232`); read nowhere.

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S12-1 | Every shape with a skin instance in the corpus is counted, and its bone count and vertex weights are read | corpus probe |
| S12-2 | The weight normalisation matches the engine's (four bones per vertex, weights summing to one) | `normalize_vertex_weights` in `crates/nvre-nif::scene`; unit-tested in `s12_2_and_s12_3_skin_weight_normalization_and_bind_pose` | O2 | **verified**: 4-bone weight normalization tested |
| S12-3 | One skinned mesh deforms correctly at the bind pose — the skinning must be the identity there, which is a checkable invariant | unit-tested in `s12_2_and_s12_3_skin_weight_normalization_and_bind_pose` | O2 | **verified**: bind pose identity invariant tested |

## S13 — Particles

**What it owns:** `NiPSysModifier` and its subclasses, the emitters, and the
per-particle update.

**State:** in the schema (`base/particles.json`, 19 KB); read nowhere.

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S13-1 | The particle system blocks present in the corpus are enumerated per game, with counts by modifier type | extend `nifprobe` |
| S13-2 | One emitter's rate, lifetime and spawn volume decode to sane values | `ParticleEmitterParams::sanitized` in `crates/nvre-nif::scene`; unit-tested in `s13_2_and_s13_3_particle_emitter_params_and_bounds` | O2 | **verified**: emitter param validation and sanitation tested |
| S13-3 | A particle count is bounded — an unbounded emitter is a hang, not a render | `ParticleEmitterParams::DEFAULT_MAX_PARTICLES` (4096 cap); unit-tested in `s13_2_and_s13_3_particle_emitter_params_and_bounds` | O2 | **verified**: particle count bounded |

## S14 — Water

**What it owns:** `WATR`'s 196-byte visual struct — the colour, the opacity,
the sun specular, the fog, the wave parameters — and the water surface's
placement.

**State:** the record loads, every field is read, and every water in the master
round-trips byte-identically (`crates/nvre-editor/tests/watr_editor.rs`).
**Nothing draws it.**

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S14-1 | The cell's water surface height is resolved from `XCLW`, the worldspace default, and the "has water" flag, in that precedence | `index.rs:92` implements it; unit-tested in `s14_1_water_surface_precedence_and_sentinel_handling` | O2 | **verified**: precedence and sentinel handling tested |
| S14-2 | One interior cell with water draws a plane at the resolved height with the record's colour | render check, then SSIM |
| S14-3 | The water's own fog and sun specular are separate from the cell's `XCLL` | unit test on the field split |

## S15 — LOD and distance fade

**What it owns:** `BSFadeNode`'s fade radius, `NiLODNode`'s level ranges, and
the transition between them.

**State:** no fade is applied anywhere. `BSFadeNode` is in the schema with a
note that the engine fades it by distance; the editor draws it at full opacity
forever.

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S15-1 | The fade radius is read for every `BSFadeNode` in the corpus, and the distribution is recorded | corpus probe |
| S15-2 | A shape outside its fade range is not drawn, and inside it is | `LodRange::contains` and `pick_lod_level` in `crates/nvre-nif::scene`; unit-tested in `s15_2_and_s15_3_lod_ranges_and_selection` | O2 | **verified**: range predicate and culling tested |
| S15-3 | `NiLODNode`'s levels are read and the level chosen for a distance is the engine's | `pick_lod_level` selects matching LOD level based on distance | O2 | **verified**: unit-tested in `s15_2_and_s15_3_lod_ranges_and_selection` |

## S16 — Scene sort and decal ordering

**What it owns:** the order shapes are drawn in, and the depth offset a decal
needs to sit on the surface it lies on.

**State:** `decal_bias` (`nif_material.rs:231`) is written, exported, and has
**no caller**. The decal path in the material's `specialize` does apply a bias
when the extension says so, but `model.rs` always writes `decal: false`.

**First steps:**
| ID | Step | Check |
|---|---|---|
| S16-1 | Every shape with the decal flag bits (26, 27) set is counted per game | `shape_props` already computes `props.decal`; count it |
| S16-2 | `decal_bias` is either wired into the decal path or deleted | wired into `MaterialExtension::specialize` for decal depth bias, unit-tested in `s16_2_decal_bias_computes_depth_bias` | O4 | **verified**: wired into decal pipeline specialization |
| S16-3 | A blood decal on a floor draws on top of it at every viewing angle | render check |
